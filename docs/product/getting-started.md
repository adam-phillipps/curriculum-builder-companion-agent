## 🚀 Key Concepts

### Learning Outcomes
Set specific, measurable learning goals that guide your educational journey. Our AI helps match you with similar objectives and suggests prerequisites.

### Gap Analysis
Discover exactly what you need to learn to reach your goals, skipping unnecessary content and focusing on knowledge gaps.

### Progress Tracking
Monitor both completion (how much content you've gone through) and comprehension (how much you actually learned).

### Interactive Learning Graph
Visualize your learning journey with hierarchical or centered layouts, filter by progress, and identify knowledge gaps.

## 🎓 Platform Benefits

### For Learners
- **Efficient Learning**: Skip content you already know
- **Personalized Pathways**: AI-generated learning routes
- **Clear Progress**: Track both completion and understanding
- **Goal-Oriented**: Focus on specific, achievable objectives

### For Educators
- **AI-Powered Tools**: Automated metadata extraction and content analysis
- **Student Insights**: Detailed progress and comprehension analytics
- **Content Optimization**: Identify gaps and improve learning materials
- **Collaboration**: Share and improve content with other educators

### For Organizations
- **Cost Effective**: Open source with no licensing fees
- **Scalable**: Handles individual learners to large institutions
- **Data-Driven**: Analytics for informed educational decisions
- **Customizable**: Adapt to your specific educational needs

## 🔧 Technical Requirements

### For Users
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Internet connection for AI features
- No software installation required

### For Administrators
- Docker and Docker Compose for deployment
- PostgreSQL database
- Redis for caching
- ChromaDB for vector search
